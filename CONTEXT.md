# Context: High Tides — Port Mouton Cottage Website

A story-driven marketing website for a single vacation-rental cottage in Port Mouton,
Nova Scotia. The site's job is to tell the story of the place and convert visitors into
**inquiries** sent to the host by email. No online payment or automated booking.

## Glossary

### Cottage
The single property the site is about. Located in Port Mouton, NS, Canada. Not beachfront,
but has a commanding view of the beach. The one and only subject of the site.
Defining facts: 2 bedrooms (2 queen beds, sleeps 4), 2 full bathrooms, full kitchen,
wood stove/fireplace, washer/dryer, AC/heat, parking, year-round availability.
Exact address is withheld on the public site and shared with a Guest only after an Inquiry.

### Hi Tides
The Cottage's name and the site's brand/wordmark. Spelled "Hi Tides" but pronounced
"high tides" — a deliberate pun (a friendly "hi" + the coastal tide). Always written
"Hi Tides" in copy. The pun is a brand asset, not a typo to be corrected.

The name exists as a real hand-painted round wooden sign mounted on the Cottage's
grey-blue cedar-shingle siding (`Content/images/logo-front.CR3`): a gold/marigold anchor
where the anchor's ring tops the "Hi" (gold), with "TIDES" below in deep purple/indigo.
This sign is the source of truth for the brand palette — gold + purple are the brand
accents; grey-blue weathered cedar is the brand's material backdrop. The site's wordmark
and palette derive from this sign, not from invented colors.

### Host
The owner of the Cottage, **Brian Greening** (inquiries → briangreening@eastlink.ca).
Receives Inquiries by email and personally follows up to arrange a stay. Loves the local
community; his personal voice and named, human presence are a core trust signal of the
site. Never named "admin" or "landlord."

### Guest
A prospective visitor who wants to stay at the Cottage. The audience of the site.

### Inquiry
A request from a Guest to stay at the Cottage, submitted through the site and delivered to
the Host by email. Contains at minimum the Guest's contact info and intended dates/party
size. An Inquiry is NOT a booking and NOT a payment — it is the start of a conversation.

### Stay
A period during which a Guest occupies the Cottage. Arranged privately between Host and
Guest after an Inquiry; the site does not track or confirm Stays. Minimum 6 nights / 7 days.
No pets. Children welcome. Rates are not published on the site — shared on request.
